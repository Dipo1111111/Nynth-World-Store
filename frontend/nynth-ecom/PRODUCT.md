# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Fashion-forward consumers in Nigeria and the diaspora, ages 18-35, seeking premium streetwear that signals identity and belonging. They discover NYNTH through social media, word of mouth, and cultural relevance. They expect a store that feels as considered as the clothing.

## Product Purpose
NYNTH is a direct-to-consumer streetwear brand. The store exists to sell products, build cultural cachet, and create a sense of exclusivity. Success is measured in revenue, brand perception, and community growth.

## Brand Personality
Minimal luxury streetwear. Confident, sharp, controlled. Think Rick Owens meets Supreme — the restraint of luxury with the edge of street culture. Black/white dominant. Typography is the design. Space is earned, not filled.

## Anti-references
- Generic Shopify templates or theme-store fashion sites
- Tech SaaS dashboards, gradient-heavy landing pages, metric-heavy hero sections
- Warm cream/beige "artisan" palettes
- Overly decorated interfaces — no ornamental borders, no glassmorphism, no gradient text
- The AI-saturated eyebrow-above-every-section pattern

## Design Principles
1. **Restraint is premium** — every element earns its place. If it doesn't serve function or brand, remove it.
2. **Typography IS the design** — spacing, weight, and tracking do the heavy lifting. Color is rare and intentional.
3. **One person designed it all** — consistency across every page. No page should feel like a different site.
4. **Sharp, not soft** — sharp corners where the brand calls for it. No default-rounding. Borders are hairline or absent.
5. **Show the product** — imagery is hero. UI recedes. The product is the loudest thing on screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast for body text (4.5:1)
- Touch targets minimum 44px on mobile
- Reduced motion support for animations
- Semantic HTML for screen readers
